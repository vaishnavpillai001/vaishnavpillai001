## 🏆 Leadership & Impact

These are the things that don't show up in a commit log — but they matter just as much.

**🔹 Owned Interview AI end-to-end at 47Billion**
From blank canvas → production system with live users. Designed the architecture, wrote the backend, handled reliability, optimized latency, fixed what broke at 2am. Every part of it.

**🔹 Led the Text-to-Lips POC**
Architected a multimodal pipeline (STT → LLM → TTS → Wav2Lip) from scratch. Researched competing approaches, evaluated quality vs. speed tradeoffs, and translated ML research into a deployable API-driven feature — all while coordinating with product and research teams.

**🔹 Cross-functional collaborator**
I don't sit in a corner and code. I work with product, research, and design teams to make sure what gets built is what actually needs to get built.

**🔹 Mentor first, engineer second**
I actively mentor junior engineers. Not because it's a checkbox — because I believe the best engineers make the people around them better.

**🔹 Intern → Engineer → Platform Owner**
47Billion hired me as an intern. I left as the person they trusted with their most ambitious product. That arc wasn't given to me. I built it.
